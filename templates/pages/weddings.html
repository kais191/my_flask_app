Weddings

The flowers, handled

We take a small number of weddings each year — usually around twenty — because the same florist who sits down with you is the one who builds your bouquet and sets up the room on the day.

Enquire about your date

What we cover

  • Bridal and bridesmaid bouquets
  • Buttonholes, corsages and flower crowns
  • Ceremony arrangements, pedestals and aisle work
  • Table centres, top-table runners and cake flowers
  • Installations — arches, hanging and entrance pieces
  • Setup, delivery and same-night strike

How it works

  1. Enquiry. Tell us your date, venue and rough budget. We'll say straight away whether we're free.
  2. Consultation. An hour in the studio, with real stems in your hands rather than a mood board on a screen.
  3. Proposal. An itemised quote within a week. Nothing is booked until you're happy with it.
  4. Deposit. 25% secures the date. The balance is due a fortnight before.
  5. The week before. A final call to confirm timings, numbers and access.
  6. The day. We deliver, set up, and come back to strike if the venue needs it.

Budget, honestly

Most of our weddings land between €1,200 and €3,500. Below about €800 you would be better served by our ready-made arrangements, and we will tell you that rather than take the booking.

Seasonality

We will always steer you towards what is genuinely good that month. Peonies in June are extraordinary; peonies in November are flown in, expensive and disappointing. A good florist tells you this before you fall in love with a photograph.
