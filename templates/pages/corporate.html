How a corporate account works

  1. A short call. We talk about the space, the budget and how often you want flowers.
  2. A trial week. We deliver one arrangement with no commitment, so you can see the standard.
  3. A standing order. If you're happy, we set a weekly or fortnightly slot and a named florist.
  4. Monthly invoicing. One invoice at the end of the month, 30-day terms.

There is no contract and no notice period. If it stops working for you, tell us and we'll stop.
