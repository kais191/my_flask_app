4. Substitutions

Because we buy fresh at market each morning, an individual flower variety may occasionally be unavailable. What we collect

When you order we collect your name, email address, phone number, the recipient's name and delivery address, and your card details. Card details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored on our systems.

Why we collect it

To take payment, make and deliver your flowers, keep you updated about the delivery, and answer you if something goes wrong. That is a contractual necessity — we cannot deliver flowers without an address.

Marketing

We only email you marketing if you have asked us to. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect immediately. We never sell or share your details with third parties for their own marketing.

Who else sees your data

Our payment provider, our delivery courier for orders outside Dublin, Kildare and Wicklow, and our email provider. Each of them sees only what they need to do their part.

How long we keep it

Order records for seven years, as Irish tax law requires. Marketing contacts until you unsubscribe. Contact-form messages for two years.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not legally required to keep it. Email {{ brand.email }} and we will respond within one month. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to remember your basket and keep you signed in. That is all — there is no advertising or analytics tracking on this site.

The seven-day freshness guarantee

Every arrangement we send is guaranteed for seven days from delivery. If your flowers wilt, droop or fail inside a week, send a photograph to {{ brand.email }} and we will replace them or refund you in full — whichever you prefer.

You do not need to return anything. You do not need the original packaging. We will not ask you to prove you changed the water.

If something arrives damaged

Tell us within 48 hours with a photograph and we will remake and redeliver the arrangement at no cost, usually the next day.

If it arrives late

If we miss the delivery date you chose and the delay is ours, we refund the delivery charge automatically. If the flowers arrive so late as to have missed the occasion entirely, we refund the order in full.

Cancelling an order

Free of charge up to 4pm the day before delivery — ring the studio and we will refund you the same day. After that point your flowers have been bought and conditioned specifically for your order.

Perishable goods are exempt from the standard 14-day cooling-off period under Irish and EU consumer law, which is why the cut-off exists. Our guarantee above is offered voluntarily and goes beyond what the law requires.

How to claim

Email {{ brand.email }} with your order reference and a photograph, or ring {{ brand.phone }}. Refunds reach your card within three to five working days.
