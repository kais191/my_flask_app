Our studio

One studio, one team, no middlemen

Nested Blooms is an independent florist in Dublin 8. We are not a relay network, and we do not pass your order to whoever happens to be nearest. Every arrangement on this site is made by our own florists, by hand, in one room, on the morning it goes out.

How we got here

We started in 2019 with a single bucket trolley on Grafton Street and a van that failed its NCT twice. The idea was simple and slightly stubborn: that people should be able to order flowers online and get something that looks like the photograph, made by a person whose name you could learn if you asked.

Six years later there are eleven of us. We still buy at the Dutch auction on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and we still buy peonies, tulips, daffodils and most of our foliage from Irish growers in Meath, Wicklow and Cork whenever the season allows it.

What we will not do

We won't send your order to a third-party florist in another county and hope for the best. We won't photograph a forty-stem bouquet and deliver a twenty-stem one. And we won't leave flowers baking on a doorstep in the sun — if nobody is home, our driver finds shade, tries a neighbour, and sends you a photograph of exactly where they left them.

The freshness guarantee

Our flowers are guaranteed for seven days. If they wilt inside a week, send us a photograph and we will replace them or refund you in full. You do not need to return anything, and we do not ask for the original packaging. In six years we have never argued with anyone about it.

Sustainability, honestly

We are not going to claim to be carbon neutral. What we can tell you is that we use no floral foam except in hatboxes and funeral tributes where there is no alternative, that our wrapping is recycled kraft paper and compostable cellophane, that our ribbons are linen or cotton, and that unsold stems go to two nursing homes in Rialto rather than into a bin.
